Node hardware

Each station pairs a shielded temperature and humidity sensor with an anemometer and a barometric sensor, read by a low-power microcontroller and published over MQTT. A solar panel and a lithium-iron cell carry the node through cloudy days and overnight.

  • SHT31 temperature and relative-humidity sensor in a radiation shield
  • BMP280 barometric pressure and altitude sensor
  • Cup anemometer with reed-switch pulse counting
  • ESP32 controller with Wi-Fi and NB-IoT fallback
  • 10 W solar panel with a 6600 mAh LiFePO4 pack

Telemetry payload

Every reading carries the node identifier, a timestamp, the coordinates and a battery figure, so a suspect series can be traced back to a specific device and power state during quality control.

{
  "node_id"   : "AWS-PNQ-02",
  "ts"        : "2026-09-02T11:45:00+05:30",
  "lat"       : 18.508,
  "lon"       : 73.926,
  "temp_c"    : 41.2,
  "rh_pct"    : 27,
  "wind_kmph" : 12.1,
  "pressure"  : 948.6,
  "battery"   : 79,
  "rssi"      : -68
}

Readings that fail a range or rate-of-change check are flagged and withheld from validation rather than silently dropped.
